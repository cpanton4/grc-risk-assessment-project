# Access Control Policy

## Purpose
This policy protects company systems and information from unauthorized access.

## Requirements
• Every employee must have an individual account.
• MFA must be enabled.
• Access must follow least privilege.
• Managers must approve access requests.
• Administrator access must be reviewed monthly.
• Departing employees must lose access immediately.
• All user access must be reviewed every three months.

## Owner
Information Security Manager

## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed every year.
