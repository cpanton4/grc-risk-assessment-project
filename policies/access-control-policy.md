# Access Control Policy

## Purpose
This policy protects company systems and information from unauthorized access by defining how user accounts, permissions, and administrative privileges are granted, reviewed, and revoked.

## Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and temporary workers who access company systems, applications, networks, or data.


## Requirements
Individual Accounts: Every employee must be assigned an individual, non‑shared user account.

Multi‑Factor Authentication (MFA): MFA must be enabled for all systems that support it.

Least Privilege: Access must be granted according to the principle of least privilege, ensuring users receive only the minimum permissions required for their job duties.

Access Approvals: All access requests must be approved by the requester’s designated Project Access Approver (fictional role).

Administrator Access Reviews: All administrator‑level access must be reviewed monthly by the System Oversight Lead (fictional role).

Quarterly User Access Reviews: All user access must be reviewed every three months by the Access Review Coordinator (fictional role).

Immediate Access Removal: Access for departing employees must be revoked immediately upon termination notification.


## Exception Process

Exceptions to this policy may be requested by submitting a written justification to the GRC Policy Exception Board (fictional role). Exceptions must document business need, duration, and compensating controls. Approved exceptions must be reviewed every 90 days.

## Owner
Information Security Manager

## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed every year.

## Incident Response Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how the company identifies, reports, and responds to security incidents to minimize impact and restore normal operations.

## Requirements
Employees must immediately report suspected security incidents to the Security Operations Team.

The Incident Response Lead must classify incidents and initiate the appropriate response procedures.

Evidence must be preserved according to forensic guidelines.

Post‑incident reviews must be completed within 10 business days.


## Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
This policy outlines acceptable use of company computers, email, internet access, and data to ensure responsible and secure behavior.

## Requirements
Company technology must be used for authorized business purposes.

Users must not engage in illegal, harmful, or disruptive activities.

Email and internet use must comply with security and confidentiality standards.

Users must not install unauthorized software or bypass security controls.
