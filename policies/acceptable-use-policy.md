 Acceptable Use Policy: 
 
 Explains how employees may use company computers, email, internet, and data.

Arthur Financial Services – Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)
Effective Date: 01/01/2026



Approved By: Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

1. Purpose
The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy is to ensure that all employees, contractors, and authorized users of Arthur Financial Services use company technology, data, and digital resources responsibly, securely, and in compliance with regulatory and business requirements. This policy supports the company’s cybersecurity risk management program and addresses risks identified in the recent cybersecurity assessment.



2. Scope
This policy applies to:

All employees (full-time, part-time, temporary)

Contractors, interns, and third-party service providers

Anyone granted access to Arthur Financial Services systems, devices, or data



3. Acceptable Use Rules
3.1 Company Devices
Company-issued Windows laptops must be used for business purposes only.

Devices must remain enrolled in Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft 365 security controls.

Users must not disable antivirus, endpoint protection, or automatic updates.

Only approved security settings may be changed; administrator privileges are restricted.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately to IT Security.



3.2 Email and Communication Tools
Company email (Microsoft 365) must be used for all business communication.

Users must not send sensitive financial or customer data to personal email accounts.

Phishing emails must be reported using the Microsoft “Report Phishing” button.

Mass emails to customers or external groups require management approval.

Email accounts must be protected with mandatory MFA, per the Critical risk treatment.



3.3 Internet Use
Internet access is provided for business activities, research, and approved training.

Users may not visit websites that contain malware, illegal content, or inappropriate material.

Streaming media unrelated to work is prohibited during business hours.

Downloading software or files from untrusted sources is not allowed.



3.4 Approved Software
Only software approved by IT Security may be installed on company devices.

Users may not install personal software, games, or browser extensions.

All software must be licensed, up to date, and managed through the company’s deployment tools.

Requests for new software must be submitted through the IT Service Desk.



3.5 Handling Company Data
Customer financial information must be stored only in approved cloud storage locations.

Data must not be saved on personal devices or unencrypted removable media.

Users must follow data classification rules and protect confidential information.

Sharing data externally requires management approval and must use secure methods.

Backups are tested every three months, and users must not interfere with backup processes.



4. Security Requirements
These requirements directly support the company’s risk treatments:

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is required for all accounts.

Offboarding procedures must be followed to ensure former employees lose access.

Privileged access reviews occur monthly; users must cooperate with audits.

Mandatory phishing and security awareness training must be completed annually.

Users must report suspicious activity immediately.



5. Reporting Security Concerns
Users must report:

Phishing attempts

Lost or stolen devices

Unauthorized access

Policy violations

Suspected data breaches

6. Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy may be granted only when:

A legitimate business need exists

The request does not introduce unacceptable risk

Compensating controls are implemented

Exception Approval Process:

User submits an exception request to IT Security.

IT Security performs a risk analysis.

The CISO reviews and approves or denies the exception.

Approved exceptions are documented and reviewed annually.



7. Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in:

Revocation of system access

Mandatory retraining

Disciplinary action up to termination

Legal action if necessary



8. Alignment With Risk Register
This policy directly addresses the following risks:

R-001: MFA required for all accounts

R-002: Offboarding process enforced

R-003: Backup testing protected from interference

R-004: Mandatory phishing training

R-005: Monthly privileged access reviews

Risk treatment priority follows the company’s rule:

Critical: Fix immediately

High: Fix soon

Medium: Monitor and plan

Low: Accept or monitor
