R-001 Risk: MFA is not enabled for every user.
Likelihood: High
Impact: High
Risk level: Critical
Treatment: Require MFA for every account.

R-002
Risk: Former employees may keep access.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk level: High
Treatment: Create a documented off boarding process.


R-003
Risk: Backups are not tested.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk level: High
Treatment: Test backups every three months.


R-004
Risk: Employees lack phishing training.
Likelihood: High
Impact: Medium
Risk level: High
Treatment: Provide mandatory security training.


R-005
Risk: Administrator access is not reviewed.
Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High
Risk level: High
Treatment: Review privileged access every month.
Simple risk rule:
• Critical: Fix immediately.
• High: Fix soon.
• Medium: Monitor and create a plan.
• Low: Accept or monitor if appropriate.
